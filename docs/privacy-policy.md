# Privacy policy — RackTrack

**Last updated:** 2026-08-15  
**Publisher:** MappM  
**Contact:** mappm.pro@gmail.com · https://github.com/Asarox33/RackTrack

This page is the publishable privacy text for Google Play.

## Who we are

RackTrack is a local American-pool scoreboard app published by **MappM**
(source repository: Asarox33/RackTrack).

## What the app stores

- Match setup preferences and completed match summaries on **your device** (local storage).
- No RackTrack user account is required for core scoring (v1 / 1.x).

## What we do not do (core scoreboard)

- We do not operate a RackTrack backend that receives your match scores for live play.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Advertising and purchases (from version 1.2.0 on store builds)

When you install a store build that includes monetization:

- **Ads:** Google AdMob may show full-screen interstitial ads when you start a new match
  or training session (not during live scoring). Ad serving may use device advertising
  identifiers and standard ad-related data as described in
  [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and AdMob disclosures.
- **Consent:** In regions that require it (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent form (UMP) may be shown
  before personalized ads are requested.
- **Remove ads:** An optional one-time in-app purchase removes ads. Payment is processed by
  Google Play (or Apple App Store on a future iOS build). Purchase status can be restored
  via the store account.

## Permissions

The app may use vibration (haptics) and keep the screen on during a match if you enable
those settings. Network access is used for ads / billing / consent when those features
are present in the build.

## Children

RackTrack is a general sports tool. Follow store age ratings and do not use the app in
ways that violate applicable children’s privacy laws.

## Changes

We may update this policy when features change (especially ads or accounts). The “Last
updated” date will change; material store changes will also update Play Data safety.

## Source code

Source is available under the RackTrack Source-Available License for viewing and study.
Official binaries are distributed through authorized app stores.
